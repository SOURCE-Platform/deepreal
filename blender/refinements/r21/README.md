# R21 side-supported drum concept

This revision studies the user's three-support layout: one fixed center
divider and one removable support at each outside end. The rear-entry arms
from the earlier concept are absent. The top of both drums is exposed. The
old rear access cuts and covers are replaced with continuous shells. The
intended assembly path is through the inner drum ends, but the inherited
shell's solid end cap has not yet been cut away under the provisional lip.

## Files

- `deepreal-side-supported-drums-r21.blend` is the editable Blender concept.
- `r21-exterior-preview.png` and `r21-front-preview.png` are review images.
- `renders/` contains additional above, travel-pose, mechanism, and drive
  views generated by `render_r21.py` (the directory is ignored by Git).
- `r21-assembly-manifest.json` lists the new parts and removed proxies.
- `r21-clearance-check.json` records sampled mesh intersections.

## Mechanism shown

Each drum is supported by a large inner bearing envelope attached to the
center divider through two recessed webs, and a smaller outer bearing and
hollow spindle at its outside cheek. A stationary motor near the center
drives a rotating internal ring gear through a pinion. A magnet on the drum
passes a fixed encoder board near the outer cheek. A moving flex proxy meets
a fixed harness at the hollow outer spindle. The drums have a 5 mm shell-end
gap, occupied in part by the 3 mm center support and its fairing.

The sampled directions are face 45° up, forward, 45° down and interaction
forward, 22.5° down, 45° down. The high, middle, and low renders show those
paired poses from above. These are review poses, not proven travel limits.
The saved render has the face drum forward and interaction drum 45° down.

## Engineering status

This is a packaging and appearance study, not a fabrication drawing. The
sampled moving shells, end parts, and races have no triangle intersections
with the fixed supports and drive envelopes at the listed poses. The check
does not certify minimum clearances, bearing loads, gear teeth, motor torque,
hard stops, wire bend life, thermal behavior, fastening, or assembly access.
The encoder board recess and mount are also schematic.

The R20 wide interaction lens and board envelopes still intersect the old
optical carrier. Their mount and the front window need a separate redesign
before a mechanically accurate or website-ready render. The exterior review
images show the design direction; materials and lighting are provisional.
The separate inner lip originally shared the old shell's outer surface with
different tessellation, producing stepped viewport marks. It is now inset
0.06 mm to prevent that display artifact. A real hollow shell and bearing
seat must still replace the overlapping concept meshes.

Regenerate from the repository root with:

```sh
blender -b -t 4 --python blender/refinements/r21/build_r21.py
blender -b -t 4 --python blender/refinements/r21/check_r21.py
blender -b -t 4 --python blender/refinements/r21/render_r21.py
```
