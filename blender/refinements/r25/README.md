# R25 exposed drum top

The drum crowns and a short section of their upper rear arcs remain exposed. The enclosure top is level with the drum crowns at 32 mm. A flat panel begins 6.5 mm behind the drum axis, covers the rear region, then curves into the vertical back. The left, center, and right supports remain part of one continuous enclosure mesh.

## Files

- `deepreal-exposed-drum-top-r25.blend` — editable model.
- `deepreal-website-presentation-r25.blend` — model with the website camera and lighting.
- `r25-website-hero-dark.png` — website render, 1800 × 1125.
- `r25-website-hero-transparent.png` — same render with alpha.
- `r25-side-preview.png` and `r25-above-preview.png` — views of the roof transition.
- `r25-enclosure-check.json` — topology and sampled pose check.

The exterior shape is a visual concept. Seams and assembly details remain for a later pass.
