# R26 contoured roof edge

The rear roof reaches forward as a tapered leading edge while leaving the drum crowns exposed. Its underside follows the upper rear drum arc with a nominal 0.5 mm radial gap. The outer supports now reveal a curved opening at the drum ends; the center island remains between the two drums. The enclosure top and drum crowns are level at 32 mm.
