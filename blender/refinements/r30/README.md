# R30 monitor-top ledge

R30 restores the stepped monitor-mount ledge to the R29 enclosure. The display-facing shelf underside is at Z = 0 mm. Its recessed back is Y = 5.5 mm and its front lip is Y = -1.5 mm, making the side-profile ledge 7.0 mm deep. The earlier geometry was 5.0 mm deep to the Y = 3.5 mm mount face.

A 40 mm wide magnet pad projects from the deeper back to the original Y = 3.5 mm magnet mounting plane. The provisional stack behind a 3.0 mm display lid remains 2.0 mm: 0.40 mm foam tape, 0.35 mm steel target, 0.35 mm air gap, and 0.90 mm device magnet. The three physical layer references and the display lid proxy are in `22 — R30 MOUNT STACK REFERENCES (HIDDEN)` in the Blender Outliner; unhide them to inspect their positions. The magnet and adhesive selections remain open.
