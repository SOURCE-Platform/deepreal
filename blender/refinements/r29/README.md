# R29 circular dividers

The three divider front edges use the drum's center and 12 mm radius, sampled at 96 segments. The outer divider faces are flush with the enclosure sides. Their lower roots extend into the body, and the dividers, partial roof, and lower enclosure form one closed mesh. The drums remain exposed on top.

Open `deepreal-circular-divider-review-r29.blend` for the assembled review scene. `deepreal-circular-divider-enclosure-r29.blend` is the source model.

- `r29-center-divider-detail.png`: close view of the center arc and its roof/body connections.
- `r29-right-side-detail.png`: close view of the outer side transition.
- `r29-assembled-preview.png`: full assembly.
- `r29-connected-shell.png`: enclosure without drums.
- `r29-enclosure-check.json`: one mesh component, zero nonmanifold edges, and no intersections in six sampled drum poses.

This remains an exterior concept. Print seams, fasteners, tolerances, and sealing have not been specified.
